Privacy Policy

Version 1 draft for website development purposes.

Last updated: September 2026

This draft explains the intended privacy principles for IPTransit-FR. It should be reviewed and adapted to the company’s actual operations, service providers, jurisdictions, and data practices before a public commercial launch.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the services enabled, IPTransit-FR may collect account information, contact information, order and delivery details, customer support communications, device information, and website usage data.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide services, process orders, communicate with customers, secure the platform, improve the website, meet legal obligations, and send marketing communications where permitted.

Payments

Future payments should be processed through reputable third-party payment providers. IPTransit-FR should avoid storing full payment card data directly unless appropriate compliance systems are in place.

Cookies

The production website may use essential, preference, analytics, and other cookies where legally permitted. Appropriate consent controls should be added where required.

Your Rights

Privacy rights vary by jurisdiction. Applicable access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, and consent withdrawal processes should be implemented before launch.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@iptransit-fr.com.

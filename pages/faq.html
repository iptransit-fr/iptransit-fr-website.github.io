Frequently asked questions.

Is IPTransit-FR accepting live orders?

Version 3 is a front-end demonstration. Cart and wishlist features work locally, but live payments and fulfillment are not connected.

How can I contact customer support?

Call +1 (212) 555-0148 during Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM ET, or email support@iptransit-fr.com.

What categories are planned?

Fiction, business, technology, education, personal growth, children’s books, and additional curated collections.

Does the cart save my books?

Yes. The demonstration cart and wishlist are stored in your browser using localStorage.

Will the store support customer accounts?

Accounts, authentication, order history, and personalized recommendations can be added in a future production version.

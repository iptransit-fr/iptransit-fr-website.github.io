Help Center

Frequently asked questions.

Is IPTransit-FR already accepting real orders?

Version 1 is a storefront demonstration. The catalog, cart, and forms work in the browser, but live payment processing and fulfillment are not connected yet.

Where is IPTransit-FR based?

The public company profile used in this Version 1 project lists New York, NY, United States.

What types of books will be available?

The planned catalog includes fiction, business, technology, education, personal development, and children’s books, with additional categories possible later.

Will international shipping be available?

The website is designed with future international growth in mind. Actual shipping regions, carriers, costs, and delivery times must be configured before launch.

Can customers create accounts?

Customer accounts, authentication, order history, and profile management are planned for a future version.

Does the cart save my selection?

Yes. Version 1 stores cart and wishlist selections locally in your browser using localStorage.
